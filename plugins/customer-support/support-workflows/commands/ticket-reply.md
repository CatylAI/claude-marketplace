Draft a reply to this support ticket: $ARGUMENTS

Before drafting, determine: what the customer is actually asking, how frustrated they are (1 to 5), and whether we can fully resolve it now, need more information, or must escalate.

Reply structure:
1. **Acknowledge** the specific problem in the customer's words, and the impact on them. One or two sentences. No "we apologise for any inconvenience".
2. **Answer or next step** — if resolvable, the exact steps, numbered. If we need information, ask for everything at once so there is no second round trip. If escalating, say who owns it and when they will hear back.
3. **Close** with one sentence that keeps the door open.

Rules: under 150 words unless steps require more; no jargon; no blaming the customer; never promise a fix date that is not confirmed. If frustration is 4 or above, offer a concrete gesture and flag the ticket for a human to review before sending.

Output the draft, then a one-line internal note: category, frustration score, escalate yes/no.
