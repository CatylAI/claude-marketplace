Prepare me for a discovery call with: $ARGUMENTS

Produce a one-page brief with:

1. **Hypothesis** — in two sentences, why this company might need what we sell and what pain we suspect they have. Label it as a hypothesis to validate, not a fact.
2. **Ten discovery questions**, ordered from broad to specific, grouped as: current situation, pain and impact, decision process, timeline and budget. Every question must be open-ended.
3. **Listen-fors** — five phrases or signals that would confirm or kill the hypothesis.
4. **Next-step options** — what I should propose at the end of the call depending on how it goes (strong fit / unclear / poor fit).

If I have notes about this prospect in the current project (search for the company name), incorporate them and cite the file. Keep the whole brief under 400 words.
