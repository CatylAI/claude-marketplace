Write a campaign brief for: $ARGUMENTS

Ask me for anything you need in a single message (product, launch date, budget band, primary channel) and then produce:

1. **Objective** — one sentence with a number in it (e.g. "500 trial signups by 30 Nov").
2. **Audience** — who exactly, what they already believe, what they need to hear.
3. **Single-minded message** — one sentence. Then three supporting proof points.
4. **Channels and assets** — a table of channel, asset, owner, due date.
5. **Success metrics** — leading indicator (we can see it in week 1) and lagging indicator (the objective).
6. **Risks and open questions** — max five bullets.

Keep it to one page. If the brand-voice skill has guidelines loaded, apply them to any copy examples.
