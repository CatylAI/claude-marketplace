Draft a job description for: $ARGUMENTS

Before writing, ask me (in one message) for anything essential that is missing: team size, what the person will own in the first 90 days, must-have vs nice-to-have skills, compensation range if we publish it.

Then produce the posting with these sections, in this order:

1. **About the role** — two short paragraphs: why this role exists and what success looks like at 12 months.
2. **What you'll do** — 5 to 7 bullets, each starting with a verb, each describing an outcome not a task.
3. **What you bring** — split into *Must have* (max 5) and *Nice to have* (max 4). Never list a degree as a must-have unless legally required.
4. **How we work** — remote policy, on-call expectations if any, interview process in one line.
5. **Compensation and benefits** — include the range if provided; otherwise leave a clearly marked placeholder.

Style rules: plain language, no "rockstar/ninja/guru", no more than one exclamation mark in the whole document, gender-neutral wording. Keep it under 450 words.
